Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026  ·  Operated by Denbigh Partners LLC

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Denbigh Partners LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Picasso ai platform, our mobile applications, and any related services (collectively, the "Services"). By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy. If you have questions, contact us at privacy@denbighpartners.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, business name, and password
  • Business information: business type, industry, number of locations, and other details provided during onboarding
  • Billing and payment information: processed securely through Stripe; we do not store card numbers
  • Communications: messages, feedback, and support requests you send to us

Financial Data via Third-Party Integrations

To provide our financial analysis Services, we connect to your business systems through authorized third-party integrations. The data collected depends on which integrations you enable, including point-of-sale platforms, accounting software, and banking connections.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and browser information: device type, operating system, browser type
  • Usage data: pages and features accessed, time spent on the platform, interaction patterns
  • Log data: IP address, access times, referring URLs, and error logs
  • Location data: general geographic location inferred from IP address (we do not collect precise geolocation)

3. Banking Data via Plaid

Picasso ai uses Plaid Technologies, Inc. to connect to your financial accounts. By using our banking integration features, you grant Denbigh Partners LLC the right, power, and authority to act on your behalf to access and transmit your personal and financial information from your relevant financial institution(s). You agree to your personal and financial information being transferred, stored, and processed by Plaid in accordance with the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

4. Accounting Data via QuickBooks

When you connect your QuickBooks Online account, we access your accounting data including chart of accounts, profit and loss statements, balance sheets, and other financial records as authorized by you and in accordance with Intuit's Developer Terms of Service. You may disconnect your QuickBooks account at any time through your account settings.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Services
  • To generate your daily Morning Brief and financial insights via AI analysis
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send transactional emails and service notifications
  • To respond to support requests
  • To detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access
  • To comply with legal obligations

Important: We do not sell your personal information or financial data. We do not use your financial data to make lending, insurance, or employment decisions. All business decisions remain with you.

6. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only with trusted service providers who perform functions on our behalf:

  • Plaid Inc. — secure bank account connectivity
  • Intuit Inc. — QuickBooks Online accounting integration
  • Stripe Inc. — payment processing
  • Auth0 (Okta) — authentication and identity management
  • Microsoft Azure — cloud infrastructure and hosting
  • Anthropic / OpenAI — AI language model processing for financial analysis

We may also disclose information if required by law, or in connection with a merger or acquisition. We will not share your information for any other purpose without your consent.

7. Data Security

  • Encryption in transit using TLS for all data communications
  • Encryption at rest using AES-256 for stored data
  • Role-based access controls ensuring only authorized personnel access data
  • Multi-tenant data isolation ensuring your data is logically separated from other users
  • OAuth 2.0 authentication with secure token management; access tokens stored server-side only
  • Audit logging of access events and administrative actions

Our infrastructure is hosted on Microsoft Azure (SOC 2 Type II certified). Our database provider Neon and identity provider Auth0 also maintain SOC 2 certifications.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services. When you close your account or request deletion, we will revoke all third-party access tokens and delete or de-identify your financial data within 30 days, retaining only what is required for legal or compliance purposes.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also disconnect any linked financial accounts or business platforms through your account settings, or manage Plaid connections directly at my.plaid.com. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@denbighpartners.com.

10. AI and Automated Processing

Picasso ai uses artificial intelligence to analyze your financial data and generate insights. Your financial data may be processed by third-party AI model providers including Anthropic and OpenAI, subject to their data processing agreements. Your data is not used by these providers to train their models. AI-generated outputs are analytical in nature and should not be considered financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to request correction, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as defined under California law. To submit a California privacy request, contact us at privacy@denbighpartners.com.

12. Children's Privacy

The Services are designed for business use and are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

13. International Users

Our Services are primarily operated from the United States. If you access the Services from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the effective date and providing notice through the Services or via email. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
